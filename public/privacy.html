Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Keepr ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via usekeepr.com and our official WhatsApp Business channels. Keepr provides an intelligent digital vault and AI personal assistant designed to safely store documents, extract important renewal deadlines, set automated reminders, and provide conversational utility.

Your privacy and document security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with Keepr.

2. Information We Collect

  • WhatsApp Contact Information: Your phone number and WhatsApp profile name provided when initiating a conversation with Keepr.
  • User Uploaded Content: Documents, photos, invoices, policies, bills, and voice notes that you explicitly send to the bot for storage or extraction.
  • Extracted Metadata: Information parsed from your documents, including document titles, issue dates, expiry/renewal dates, policy or bill numbers, and amounts due.
  • Transaction Data: If you purchase a subscription plan, payment processing is handled securely via RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay). We never store your card numbers or UPI MPIN.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide and improve the Keepr service:

  • To securely store and retrieve your personal documents and photos on demand.
  • To analyze documents using artificial intelligence to extract renewal dates and policy details.
  • To send you proactive WhatsApp reminders before important document expiries and deadlines.
  • To respond to your natural-language searches, voice queries, and customer support inquiries.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or uploaded documents to any third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security & Storage

All uploaded documents are encrypted both in transit (via HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and at rest (using AES-256 bit encryption). User data is hosted on enterprise-grade cloud database infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring that only your verified WhatsApp account can access your files.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver seamless functionality, we integrate with trusted enterprise platforms:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API): For delivering secure, end-to-end encrypted messaging over the official WhatsApp network.
  • Google Cloud & Gemini AI: For multimodal document intelligence, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and voice transcription.
  • Supabase Inc.: For encrypted cloud database and object storage.
  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.: For processing subscription payments.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your data. You may at any time request the permanent deletion of your account, chat history, and all stored documents by typing "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" in the WhatsApp chat or by emailing us at care@usekeepr.com. Upon receipt of your request, all files are purged within 24 hours.
