Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

RoasSiren collects only information necessary to deliver ad stock monitoring and emergency WhatsApp siren notifications:

  • Phone Numbers: WhatsApp mobile numbers provided to receive siren alerts.
  • Monitored URLs: Public Shopify product pages and ad landing pages submitted for 24/7 radar scanning.
  • Audit Diagnostics: Response times, HTTP status codes, variant inventory counts, and notification timestamps.

2. Zero Sensitive Store Access

RoasSiren does not require Shopify admin credentials, customer order details, or private billing data. All monitoring is conducted strictly via publicly accessible web endpoints and native JSON interfaces.

3. How We Use Information

We use your submitted data exclusively to execute periodic stock audits, calculate ROAS waste estimates, and dispatch WhatsApp sirens via our Meta Cloud API integration. We never sell or share contact details with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Deletion

You can remove any monitored URL or delete your phone number from our radar at any time by messaging stop <url> on WhatsApp or contacting siren@keepr-bot.onrender.com.
