Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Strictly DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

1. Introduction & Commitment

Welcome to Keepr ("we", "our", or "us"), operating at https://keepr-bot.onrender.com and through our official WhatsApp Business channels. Keepr is an autonomous AI life vault and smart assistant engineered to securely store documents, track renewal deadlines, set automated reminders, and provide conversational utility.

Your privacy and document security are our highest priority. This Privacy Policy sets forth how your personal data is collected, processed, encrypted, and protected in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

2. Information We Collect

  • WhatsApp Identification: Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name sent when you message our verified line (+91 98705 30066).
  • User-Uploaded Documents & Media: Documents, photos, invoices, vehicle registration certificates (RC), insurance policies, bills, and voice notes that you explicitly upload to the vault.
  • Extracted Metadata: Information parsed via optical character recognition and Gemini 2.0 Vision, including policy/registration numbers, issue/expiry dates, and due amounts.
  • Payment Transactions: Subscription payments are processed via RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay). We never capture or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI MPINs.

3. How We Use & Protect Your Information

We process your data strictly to deliver the requested services:

  • To store and return your exact uncompressed original files on demand.
  • To schedule proactive WhatsApp alerts before document expiration (30, 7, and 1 day prior).
  • To calculate personalized 6:00 AM daily guidance based on your provided date of birth.
Security Guarantee: All files are encrypted using military-grade AES-256 GCM encryption. Your personal files and conversations are NEVER used to train public AI models, nor sold to third-party telemarketers or insurance brokers.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you retain absolute ownership of your data. You may request immediate deletion of all your stored files, extracted metadata, and conversation history at any time by messaging /delete on WhatsApp or emailing care@keepr-bot.onrender.com.
