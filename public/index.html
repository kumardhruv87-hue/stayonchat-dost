No! Keepr runs 100% inside your existing WhatsApp. Just click any green button or text +91 98705 30066. There are no passwords to remember and no apps eating your phone storage.

Yes, absolutely! When you drop a PDF (like an insurance policy, car RC, or invoice), Keepr stores the exact original file in your encrypted vault. When you ask "Send my RC", Keepr sends the original PDF file back natively in WhatsApp.

When you upload a document, Gemini 2.0 Vision automatically extracts the expiry date (e.g., insurance expiry: 14 Oct 2026). Keepr schedules automated reminders at 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day prior, sending you a proactive WhatsApp alert with renewal details.
